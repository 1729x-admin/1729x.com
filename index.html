Manifesto

Most monasteries became the world they renounced, with rank, ritual and costume. We keep only one thing: an atmosphere of truth, guarded so that nothing false survives in it.

Iसत्य

Truth alone

No rulebook, rituals or rank. Truth is the only allegiance, above tribe, comfort and applause.

IIद्वार

Not for everyone

The door is open to the sincere and closed to performers. Credentials count for nothing.

IIIअर्थ

In the world

Not a retreat. We build and create value, because reality is the hardest test of truth.

IVविद्या

Knowledge is sacred

Sacred because it is guarded, not because of lineage. For Bharat, and for humanity.

Why 1729x

For the Ramanujans not yet found.

1729 is Ramanujan's number. He was a self-taught clerk from Madras with no degree, and his genius only needed to be found.

The x is the unknown: the minds, in any field, that the mainstream hasn't reached yet. We are dedicated to them.
